About Our Firm

Founded in 1994, First Capital Investment Realty is a family-owned, full-service real estate brokerage with offices in Beverly Hills, West Hills, and Claremont. For over 35 years, we have served investors, property owners, home buyers, and sellers throughout the greater Los Angeles and Southern California markets — specializing in commercial investments, residential sales, multifamily properties, and value-add opportunities.

Our approach combines deep local market knowledge with institutional-grade analysis, giving our clients the insight and confidence to make informed investment decisions. We pride ourselves on personal service, integrity, and a long-term commitment to each client relationship.
